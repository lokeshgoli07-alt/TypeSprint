Touch Typing Mastery Guide

Fundamental techniques to double your typing speed and eliminate strain.

1. The Home Row Anchor

Always return your index fingers to the home row keys with small physical tactile bumps: F (Left Hand) and J (Right Hand).

A S D F · · · J K L ;
Pinky Ring Middle Index

2. Golden Practice Rules

  • Accuracy Before Speed: Speed is a natural byproduct of precision. Aim for 96%+ accuracy.
  • Eyes on the Screen: Never look down at your keyboard. Trust your fingers' spatial memory.
  • Consistent Rhythm: Type like a metronome with uniform cadence rather than bursting and pausing.
  • Ergonomic Posture: Keep your wrists slightly elevated and your back straight to prevent strain.

3. Quick Keyboard Shortcuts

Esc Instant restart of the current test
Alt + P Pause / Resume the active test
Space Resume when test is paused
